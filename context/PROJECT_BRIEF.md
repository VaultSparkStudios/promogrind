# Project Brief

## Identity

- Name: PromoGrind
- Slug: promogrind
- Type: Public web application
- Status: FORGE · launch hardening · public-unlaunched
- Owner: VaultSpark Studios LLC

## Why this exists

- Problem or opportunity: Sportsbook promotions are fragmented, time-sensitive, and easy to evaluate badly without a coherent operating system.
- Audience: Disciplined users who want to compare promotions, manage bankroll exposure, track execution, and improve decisions without gambling hype.
- Studio importance: PromoGrind is the studio's calm, high-signal operator product for proving that promotion intelligence can be useful, evidence-led, and commercially sustainable.

## Success

- What success looks like: Users can move from discovery through calculation, execution, and retrospective learning with clear tradeoffs, trustworthy records, and no hidden risk claims.
- What this project is not: A casino-style dopamine product, a promise of guaranteed income, or a shallow affiliate calculator farm.

## Scope

- In scope: Promotion discovery, calculators, bankroll and risk tooling, workflow memory, sober AI assistance, account/subscription surfaces, and launch-proof automation.
- Out of scope: Sportsbook operation, wagering custody, financial advice, fabricated profitability claims, and public launch claims without production evidence.
