# Soul

## Core promise

PromoGrind is the free tool that does the math sportsbooks don't want you to do. It takes promotions that look complicated and turns them into a clear number: here is how much you will walk away with. No fluff. No subscription. No guessing.

## Non-negotiables

- Must always be true: The math is correct. Every calculator output is verifiable. Users can trust the numbers.
- Audience should feel: Confident, in control, and like they have an unfair advantage that's completely legal.
- Quality bar: Fast, clean, mobile-friendly. No ads cluttering the experience. No dark patterns. The tool earns trust by being genuinely useful.

## Anti-goals

- Must never drift into: Gambling encouragement. This is a math and analytics tool, not a gambling promotion.
- Cheap imitation to avoid: Clunky spreadsheet vibes, overwhelming UI, or paywalling basic functionality.
- Tonal failure mode: Bro-y gambling hype, misleading profit claims, or anything that sounds like it was written by a sportsbook marketing team.
