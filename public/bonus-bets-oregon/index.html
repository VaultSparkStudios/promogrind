Frequently Asked Questions

Is sports betting legal in Oregon?
Yes, since 2019 via the Oregon Lottery. Only DraftKings (licensed through the lottery) and the state's Scoreboard app are available. Must be 21+.
Why is only DraftKings available in Oregon?
Oregon chose a state-lottery controlled model where the lottery selects a single private operator (DraftKings) rather than allowing open competition. This limits promo variety vs multi-operator states.
Can I use FanDuel in Oregon?
No. FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, and all other operators besides DraftKings and Scoreboard are not licensed in Oregon as of 2026.
