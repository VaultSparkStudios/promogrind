What Is a Bonus Bet?

A bonus bet — also called a free bet — is a credit awarded by a sportsbook that lets you place a wager without risking your own money. The key difference from a real cash bet: if you win, you receive the profit only — your original stake is not returned. A $100 bonus bet on +300 odds returns $300 in profit, not $400.

Why Hedge a Bonus Bet?

Because the stake is not returned on a win, using a bonus bet on a heavy favorite is inefficient — you win very little. The smarter play is to bet the bonus on an underdog and place a smaller cash bet on the opposing side at another book. This locks in a modeled return regardless of which outcome wins, a technique called bonus bet conversion or free bet hedging.

Both prices determine the conversion

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a bonus bet?
A bonus bet is a promotional wager credit from a sportsbook where only the profit (not the stake) is paid out on a win. They are commonly offered as sign-up incentives, reload bonuses, and bet-and-get promotions.
How do I convert a bonus bet to cash?
Enter the bonus amount and both current prices. The calculator compares conditional outcomes, assuming both wagers are accepted at those prices under matching settlement rules. A rejected, limited or voided wager can cause a loss.
What odds should I use?
There is no universal best price range. Compare both current prices and the full terms; the calculator models those inputs, not a promised result.
