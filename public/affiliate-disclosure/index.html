Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: April 3, 2026

Short version: Some links on PromoGrind are affiliate links. If you create a new sportsbook account through one of our links, we may earn a referral commission — at no cost to you. Our calculator math is never influenced by any affiliate relationship.

What This Disclosure Covers

In accordance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), PromoGrind discloses all material connections to the sportsbooks and services featured on this platform.

How Our Affiliate Relationships Work

PromoGrind may earn a commission when a user:

  • Clicks a sportsbook link on our site and creates a new account at that sportsbook
  • Qualifies for a new-user promotion after registering through our affiliate link

These are Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) or revenue-share arrangements with licensed, legal US sportsbooks. The commission is paid by the sportsbook — it never increases the cost of any promotion or service for you.

Our Integrity Policy

Affiliate relationships do not influence:

  • Calculator math: Every formula, conversion rate, and calculation PromoGrind performs is mathematically correct regardless of which sportsbooks are featured. The math is the product.
  • Editorial content: Our Knowledge Base, Glossary, and educational content are written to be accurate, not to favor any particular sportsbook.
  • Promo rankings or recommendations: We do not rank or order promotions based on commission rates.

No Additional Cost to You

You will never pay more for a sportsbook account, promotion, or subscription because you arrived through a PromoGrind affiliate link. In many cases, our links include exclusive welcome offers that you may not receive by signing up directly.

FTC Compliance
