Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

PromoGrind ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by VaultSpark Studios, is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. For a detailed breakdown of every data type we collect, see our Data Use Policy.

1. Information We Collect

No-account use: If you use the calculators without creating an account, no personal data is collected or sent to our servers. Calculator inputs remain in your browser only.

Account Information: When you create a free PromoGrind account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. We do not store plain-text passwords. Studio membership is separate from PromoGrind account creation.

Usage Data: We may use PostHog analytics and Sentry error monitoring to understand which pages and features are used, diagnose crashes, and improve product reliability. PromoGrind disables automatic interaction capture by default, masks session replay text and inputs where replay is enabled, and does not use analytics for advertising or resale.

Ledger & Tracker Data: Your bet history, P/L entries, sportsbook tracker data, and promo tracking information are stored in our database to enable cloud sync. This data belongs to you and can be exported or deleted at any time.

Community Content: If you submit content to community features (Wins Wall, Community Promos, Leaderboard), that content may be visible to other users per the settings you choose.

Communications: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email and send you emails via Resend. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Payment Information: Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card number. We receive subscription status from Stripe via webhook.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the PromoGrind service
  • To sync your data across devices when you're signed in
  • To send the newsletter or transactional emails if you've opted in
  • To send push notifications if you've enabled them
  • To process payments via Stripe for VaultSparked Pro
  • To display community content you've chosen to share publicly
  • To improve the product using privacy-conscious analytics and error monitoring

3. Third-Party Services

Supabase: Authentication, database storage, and backend functions. See supabase.com/privacy.

Stripe: Subscription payment processing. See stripe.com/privacy.

Resend: Email delivery (newsletter, transactional). See resend.com/privacy.

PostHog: Product analytics and feature usage measurement when enabled. PromoGrind disables automatic interaction capture, does not use PostHog for advertising, and masks replay text and inputs. See posthog.com/privacy.

Sentry: Error monitoring and crash diagnostics when enabled in production. See sentry.io/privacy.

Anthropic (Claude): Powers AI features (PromoChat, Promo Advisor, AI Action Plan). Promo Advisor removes supported direct identifiers before analysis. Bankroll and active-book context stay in your browser unless you explicitly select the personalization checkbox; when selected, only those named fields are included. Privacy receipts store redaction counts and field names, not the removed values. See anthropic.com/privacy.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use advertising cookies or sell tracking data. We use browser localStorage for app preferences, offline data cache, and Supabase auth session tokens. When product analytics are enabled, PostHog may use localStorage and first-party cookies to measure product usage and prevent duplicate events.

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Deleted accounts are removed within 30 days. Aggregated analytics and error-monitoring data may be retained for product-quality and security analysis.

6. Your Rights

  • Access & Export: Export your ledger and tracker data as CSV from within the app.
  • Deletion: To delete your account and all data, email support@vaultsparkstudios.com.
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from emails at any time using the unsubscribe link or app settings.

7. California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know, right to delete, and right to opt out of sale of personal information.

Do Not Sell My Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for commercial purposes. We never have.

To exercise CCPA rights, email privacy@vaultsparkstudios.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject. We respond within 45 days. For more detail, see our Data Use Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

PromoGrind is intended for users 21 years of age and older (18+ in states where permitted). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately.

9. Data Security

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Supabase uses row-level security so your data is only accessible to you. In the event of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top reflects when changes were made. Material changes will be communicated to registered users. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

General support: support@vaultsparkstudios.com
