Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

PromoGrind ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by VaultSpark Studios, is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. We do not store plain-text passwords.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the app — which calculators you use, how often you visit, and basic session data. This is used to improve the product.

Ledger & Tracker Data: Your bet history, P/L entries, sportsbook tracker data, and related promo tracking information are stored in our database to enable cloud sync across your devices. This data belongs to you.

Communications: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email and frequency preference. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Payment Information: If you subscribe to VaultSparked Pro, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number. We receive confirmation of subscription status from Stripe.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the PromoGrind service
  • To sync your data across devices when you're signed in
  • To send you the newsletter if you've opted in
  • To send push notifications if you've enabled them
  • To process payments via Stripe for VaultSparked Pro
  • To improve the product using aggregated, anonymized usage analytics

3. Third-Party Services

Supabase: We use Supabase for authentication, database storage, and backend functions. Your account and tracker data are stored on Supabase-managed infrastructure. See supabase.com/privacy.

Stripe: Subscription payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. See stripe.com/privacy.

Resend: Newsletter and transactional emails are sent via Resend. See resend.com/privacy.

Plausible Analytics: We use Plausible for privacy-friendly website analytics. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. See plausible.io/privacy.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is removed from our systems within 30 days. Anonymized, aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You can export your ledger and tracker data from within the app (CSV export).
  • Deletion: To delete your account and all associated data, email us at support@vaultsparkstudios.com.
  • Opt-out: You can unsubscribe from newsletters at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by updating your preferences in the app.

6. Cookies

We use browser localStorage to save your calculator preferences, ledger data (offline cache), and app settings. We do not use advertising cookies. Supabase authentication uses a session token stored in localStorage.

7. Children's Privacy

PromoGrind is intended for users 21 years of age and older (18+ in states where permitted). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will post the updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@vaultsparkstudios.com.
