What Is 3-Way Arbitrage?

Three-way arbitrage covers markets where there are three possible outcomes: home win, draw, and away win. Soccer match result markets are the most common example. By placing bets on all three outcomes across different sportsbooks — where each book has priced a different outcome more generously — the combined implied probabilities can fall below 100%, creating a guaranteed profit regardless of which team wins or whether the game ends in a draw.

Why 3-Way Arb Is Harder Than 2-Way

Standard 2-way arb requires only two sportsbooks. A 3-way arb requires three different books offering competitive prices on three different outcomes simultaneously. The coordination window is tighter — odds move quickly, and you must place all three bets before any line shifts. Additionally, larger total stakes are spread across three books instead of two, requiring more capital to generate the same dollar profit.

How to Calculate 3-Way Stakes

Convert each outcome's American odds to decimal, then compute the margin formula: sum the inverse of each decimal odd (1/odds1 + 1/odds2 + 1/odds3). If this sum is less than 1.0, an arb exists. The arb percentage is (1 − sum) × 100. To find each bet's stake: multiply your total bankroll by each outcome's implied probability divided by the total margin. The Margin Formula

The margin (also called overround or juice) of a 3-way market is: (1/decimal_odds_home) + (1/decimal_odds_draw) + (1/decimal_odds_away) − 1. A typical sportsbook 3-way market has a margin of 5–8%. When you find a combination where this calculation produces a negative result — meaning the sum is below 1.0 — the difference is your guaranteed ROI on that arb.

Typical ROI on 3-Way Arbs

Most 3-way arb opportunities produce between 0.5% and 2% ROI on total stakes. This is smaller than many 2-way arbs but still risk-free profit. A 1% arb on $3,000 total stakes (split across three books) yields $30 in guaranteed profit. How do I calculate 3-way arbitrage?

Is 3-way arb more profitable than 2-way?
