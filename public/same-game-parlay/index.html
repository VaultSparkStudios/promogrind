How Are SGP Odds Calculated?

A same game parlay combines correlated legs from the same event (e.g., a QB to throw 300+ yards and his team to win). Standard parlay math multiplies independent probabilities, but SGPs involve correlation adjustments — the book's proprietary correlation model is unknown. The estimator uses a conservative independence assumption to give you a baseline fair-odds floor.

When Are SGP Boosts +EV?

SGP profit boosts (e.g., "25% profit boost on any 3-leg SGP") can push the boosted odds above fair value. Enter the fair odds, your stake, and the boost percentage — the calculator shows whether the boosted payout exceeds the expected cost of the bet. Most SGP boosts have max win caps that also affect EV; those are factored in.

SGP vs. Regular Parlay: Key Differences

Regular parlays use published straight-bet odds with a fixed parlay payout table. SGPs use the book's internal correlation model — which typically results in shorter odds than a regular parlay would give you on the same legs. That's why comparing the SGP price to the regular parlay price is a useful vig check.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which sportsbooks offer same game parlays?
DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, bet365, and ESPN BET all offer SGPs. Each book uses a different correlation model, so the same leg combination may be priced differently across books — always shop the line.
Can I hedge an SGP?
Hedging an SGP after it goes live (with legs hitting) is a classic strategy. Use the PromoGrind Parlay Hedge Calculator to determine the exact hedge stake needed to model a return or minimize loss on a live SGP.
Are SGPs good for matched betting?
Generally no — the correlation penalty makes SGPs worse than straight bets for matched betting purposes. However, SGP boosts can create +EV opportunities when the promotion exceeds the correlation discount.
