4. Cookies and Local Storage

PromoGrind does not use advertising cookies or sell tracking data.

  • Browser localStorage: We use localStorage to save your calculator preferences, app settings, and offline cache of your ledger/tracker data. This stays on your device.
  • Supabase auth token: When you log in, Supabase stores an auth session token in localStorage (key prefix: sb-). This is how your session persists between visits.
  • Product analytics: When enabled, PostHog may use localStorage and first-party cookies to measure product usage and avoid duplicate events. PromoGrind disables automatic interaction capture and masks replay text and inputs.

6. Your Rights

All Users

  • Data access: Export your ledger and tracker data as CSV from within the app
  • Account deletion: Email support@vaultsparkstudios.com to delete your account and all associated data within 30 days
  • Newsletter opt-out: Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or update preferences in the app

California Residents (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have additional rights:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information we have collected (subject to certain exceptions)
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information. There is nothing to opt out of.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights
Do Not Sell My Personal Information

VaultSpark Studios does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their commercial purposes. We never have and we don't intend to.

To exercise any CCPA rights, email privacy@vaultsparkstudios.com with the subject "CCPA Request". We will respond within 45 days.

EU/UK Residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have rights under GDPR/UK GDPR including:

  • Right of access to your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Note: PromoGrind is primarily a US market product. If you are located in the EU/UK, you use it at your own discretion. Email privacy@vaultsparkstudios.com for GDPR requests.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Supabase uses row-level security (RLS) so your data is only accessible to you
  • Passwords are hashed by Supabase's auth system — we never see plain-text passwords
  • Community content (wins wall, community promos) is moderated before public display

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

8. User-Generated Content

If you submit content to community features (Community Promos, Wins Wall, Leaderboard), note that:

  • Community Promos are public and visible to all users
  • Leaderboard participation is opt-in and can be toggled off in your privacy settings
  • Wins Wall entries you mark as public are visible to all users
  • By submitting public content, you grant VaultSpark Studios a non-exclusive license to display and distribute that content as part of the PromoGrind service
  • To remove your public content, contact support@vaultsparkstudios.com

9. Children's Privacy

PromoGrind is not intended for users under 18 years of age (21+ in most US states). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered an account, contact us immediately at support@vaultsparkstudios.com.
