Sports Betting Montana

Montana legalized sports betting in 2019 using a state-lottery controlled model. Only the Sports Bet Montana app (operated via the Montana Lottery) is licensed. Major commercial operators like DraftKings, FanDuel, and BetMGM are NOT available in Montana.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DraftKings available in Montana?
No. DraftKings is not licensed in Montana. The state uses a lottery-controlled model with Sports Bet Montana as the only licensed mobile app.
What is Sports Bet Montana?
Sports Bet Montana is the official sports betting app of the Montana Lottery, operated in partnership with Scientific Games. It's the only legal mobile sports betting option in Montana.
When will Montana allow major sportsbooks?
There's no current movement to open Montana's market to commercial operators. The state appears committed to its lottery model for now. Check the PromoGrind blog for Montana regulatory updates.
