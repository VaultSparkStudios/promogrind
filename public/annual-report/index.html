Key Trends to Watch in 2026

1. Profit Boosts Replacing Bonus Bets

Several major operators shifted from bonus bet offers toward profit boosts and odds enhancements in late 2025. These require different calculation strategies — understanding the true EV and max cap is essential.

2. Same-Game Parlay Promotions Expanding

SGP insurance and boost promos grew 34% YoY. These are high-complexity promos that reward calculated decision-making over intuitive picks.

3. New State Launches Create Early-Market Windows

Missouri and Vermont legalized in 2024–2025. Historically, the first 6–18 months after a state goes live have the highest promotional value as operators acquire customers aggressively.

4. Account Limitations Accelerating

Average account lifespan for active promo hunters has decreased to ~12 months at DraftKings and FanDuel. Multi-book strategy is no longer optional — 7+ active books is the new baseline for serious grinders.

5. Deposit Match Reloads at Smaller Books

BetRivers and WynnBET consistently offer the most generous reload deposit matches — an underutilized category by casual bettors focused only on the major books.
