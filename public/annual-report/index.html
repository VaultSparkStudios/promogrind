Evidence before benchmarks.

PromoGrind does not currently publish an annual industry-performance report. An earlier page at this address used illustrative aggregate figures without a sourceable dataset; those figures were removed on July 31, 2026.

Current standard

Three claims, kept mechanically distinct

Modeled scenario: math calculated from named user inputs and assumptions. Realized outcome: an actual settled value recorded by the user. Aggregate benchmark: published only when its dataset, sample, period, method, and limitations can be inspected.

What you can verify now

Use the conversion calculators for an input-specific model, then record the actual result in the ledger. Prices, limits, rejected stakes, partial fills, voids, grading differences, eligibility, taxes, and execution can reduce or reverse a modeled return.

This correction is intentional: a missing benchmark is more useful than a precise-looking number without defensible provenance.
