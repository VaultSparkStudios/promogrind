How to Convert a UK Free Bet to Cash

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a bonus bet and a free bet in the UK?
They are the same thing. "Free bet" is the common UK term; "bonus bet" is the US equivalent. In both cases, the stake is not returned on a winning bet — only the profit is paid out.
Why use a calculator for free bets?
The lay stake at the exchange must be precisely calculated to lock in profit regardless of outcome. Too much or too little, and you either risk a loss or leave guaranteed profit on the table. The calculator does this instantly.
Do UK free bet offers expire?
Yes. Most UK free bets expire within 7–30 days of being credited. Check the offer terms at each bookmaker. The PromoGrind Promo Calendar tracks expiry dates for major UK offers.
