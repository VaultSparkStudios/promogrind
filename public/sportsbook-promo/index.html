The Main Promo Types Explained

Welcome offers are the highest-value promotions available. They are one-time offers that trigger when you create a new account and make your first deposit or qualifying bet. Common formats include first bet insurance (refund up to $X as bonus bets if your first bet loses) and bet-and-get offers ($5 qualifying bet earns $200 in bonus bets instantly).

Profit boosts (also called odds boosts) increase the payout on a specific bet by a fixed percentage — for example, 50% profit boost on a moneyline. They reduce the sportsbook's margin and can be +EV if applied to the right bets. PromoGrind's profit boost calculator tells you the effective vig after the boost.

Insurance bets refund your stake as a bonus bet if a specific condition is met — usually if your bet loses on the last leg of a parlay or within a specific scoring margin. Treat these as a partial risk reduction, not a guarantee.

Reload bonuses and deposit matches are periodic offers that give you bonus funds or betting credits tied to a new deposit. Common during major sporting events: Super Bowl, March Madness, and playoffs.
