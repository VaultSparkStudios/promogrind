How Matched Betting Works — Complete 2026 Guide

Matched betting uses opposing wagers to model the cash value of an offer. It still involves gambling and real money. A calculation can balance two outcomes under stated assumptions; it cannot guarantee that both wagers will be accepted or settled as modeled.

Start with the offer terms

Check eligibility, expiry, minimum odds, qualifying costs, stake limits and settlement rules. Confirm whether a bonus stake is returned with winnings. Different rules produce different results.

Worked example: a bonus stake that is not returned

This is a hypothetical two-outcome market, not a current sportsbook offer. A $200 bonus at +350 (4.50 decimal) pays $700 in winnings if it wins. The opposite cash wager at −400 (1.25 decimal) needs a $560 stake to balance the modeled outcomes.

Hypothetical $200 bonus
Bonus wins$700 winnings − $560 lost hedge = $140
Hedge wins$560 × 0.25 = $140 cash profit
Modeled conversion$140 ÷ $200 = 70%

The $560 cash stake must be available. This example excludes qualifying losses, fees, tax, rounding, market changes and settlement differences. A rejected, limited or voided leg can create a loss.

Review before making a decision

  1. Read the current operator terms and confirm you are eligible.
  2. Enter the actual bonus amount and both current odds into the calculator.
  3. Compare both outcomes, cash required and costs. Do not treat the modeled return as realized profit.
  4. Check that the wagers cover the same event and all outcomes under matching settlement rules.
  5. Choose whether to proceed or skip. Record any accepted wagers and their eventual settlement.

Why conversion rates vary

Both the bonus odds and opposite cash odds matter. A higher bonus price alone does not guarantee a better conversion. Use the exact pair of odds rather than a typical earnings range.

Profit boosts

A profit boost changes the profit portion of a winning wager, subject to the offer's cap and restrictions. Enter the boost, stake and opposite odds in the Profit Boost Calculator to compare modeled outcomes. A boost does not automatically create a positive return.

Limits of the model

Odds movement, cash requirements, rejected wagers, voids, account restrictions and taxes can change the result. Verify the operator's terms and local rules. PromoGrind does not promise income or identify a universal best price range.
