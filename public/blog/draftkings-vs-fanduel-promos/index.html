PromoGrind field guide

Compare the offer in front of you—not a stale brand average.

DraftKings and FanDuel promotions vary by jurisdiction, account, date, eligibility, sport, limit, and operator terms. PromoGrind therefore does not publish a typical monthly-profit table or declare a permanent winner.

Use the same evidence packet for both books

  1. Capture the exact offer text, expiry, minimum odds, cap, credit type, playthrough, and excluded markets.
  2. Record current prices for both settlement legs and the maximum accepted stake.
  3. Run the matching PromoGrind calculator; save assumptions and missing inputs.
  4. Before execution, re-price both legs and check void/grading rules.
  5. After settlement, record realized profit or loss, time, friction, and whether the model was accurate.

A defensible comparison

Prefer the operator whose verified, eligible offers produce better realized results for your constraints after time, capital exposure, limits, and execution failures. A larger headline bonus or a higher modeled return is not sufficient evidence.
