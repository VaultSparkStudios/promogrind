PromoGrind evidence method

“Best promo” is a live decision, not a permanent leaderboard.

Offer terms rotate and may be personalized. A ranking without a current source, capture time, jurisdiction, eligibility scope, and reproducible calculation is not trustworthy. PromoGrind does not display one here.

01

Verify

Capture operator terms, region, expiry, minimum odds, cap, credit type, playthrough, exclusions, and the time observed.

02

Model

Use current prices and accepted stake limits. Label assumptions, missing inputs, sensitivity triggers, and execution risks.

03

Re-price

Check both legs immediately before acting. Price movement, partial acceptance, voids, and grading differences can change the result.

04

Learn

Record realized profit or loss, time, friction, and model accuracy. Rank your own eligible offers from evidence rather than brand reputation.

Why the old ranking disappeared

Earlier content at this URL used unsourced offer-frequency and monthly-return ranges. PromoGrind removed those figures on July 31, 2026. Until a live, timestamped, sourceable feed supports a ranking, an honest method is better than fabricated currency.
