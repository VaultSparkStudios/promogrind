FAQ

Is there a minimum follower count?
No. We accept creators at any size. What matters is that your audience includes sports bettors, matched bettors, or promo hunters. A 500-subscriber YouTube channel with the right audience outperforms a 50K channel with a general audience.
What platforms qualify?
YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, podcasts, Discord servers, Reddit, Twitch, newsletters, and blogs. If you create content that sports betting enthusiasts consume, you qualify.
Can I use the tool in my videos?
Absolutely — that's the point. Record your screen using PromoGrind to walk through a real promo. The tool is free, so viewers can immediately follow along. This is the highest-converting content type.
How long does the 30% last?
12 months from each subscriber's signup date. So subscribers you refer in January pay through January of the following year. There's no cap on how many subscribers you can refer.
When and how do I get paid?
Payments on the 1st of each month for the previous month's earnings. Minimum payout $25. PayPal or US bank transfer. International creators: PayPal only.
