What PromoGrind Does

PromoGrind is a math calculator for sportsbook promotional offers — like a mortgage calculator for bonus bets. You enter numbers, it outputs conversion values, EV, hedge amounts, and profit projections. It does not place bets, access sportsbook accounts, or handle money of any kind.
