For legal or privacy requests, include the relevant page URL and the email tied to your PromoGrind account when applicable. Do not send payment card numbers or sportsbook account credentials.

What To Include

Account or billing help

Describe the sign-in, subscription, checkout, portal, or recovery step that failed. Include screenshots only after removing sensitive details.

Calculator accuracy

Send the calculator name, inputs, book odds format, expected result, and the result PromoGrind showed so we can reproduce it.

Privacy or deletion

Use the subject line "Privacy Request" and include the PromoGrind account email. We respond under the timelines listed in the privacy policy.

Partnerships or affiliates

Include the sportsbook, creator, or publisher name, the relevant offer URL, and any compliance requirements.
